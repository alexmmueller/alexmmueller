### Hi, I’m Alex.

I first dialled up in 1990 and was instantly hooked by the internet’s promise: anyone could learn, connect, and participate. I’ve since watched platforms concentrate power and turn users into products.

**[Protocols over platforms](https://www.knightcolumbia.org/content/protocols-not-platforms-a-technological-approach-to-free-speech)** has guided my work across network analytics, security, and crypto. Today, I’m exploring how AI can serve people without concentrating power further.

Here you’ll find experiments in agent monitoring, model evaluation, and interpretability, alongside contributions to open-source infrastructure.
